Python Streamlit Machine Learning MRP Pandas Scikit-learn

ML-Powered Forecasting & MRP Tool

A comprehensive supply chain planning application that integrates machine learning-based demand forecasting with full Material Requirements Planning (MRP) functionality. Built to replace complex Excel-based systems with an interactive, user-friendly web interface.

Key Capabilities:

  • Data Integration: Upload and process Excel/CSV files with automatic schema detection
  • ML Forecasting: Time series models for demand prediction with configurable parameters
  • Automated MRP: Full bill-of-materials explosion, net requirements calculation, and planned order generation
  • Inventory Optimization: Safety stock recommendations based on demand variability and lead times
  • Interactive Dashboards: Visual KPIs including inventory turns, forecast accuracy, and stock coverage
